# Princess and the Prepo — Locked Story Flow

This product must use one connected retention loop:

1. Word Scattering
2. Sentence Reconstruction
3. Picture Matching
4. Digital Storybook
5. Story Questions based only on events shown in the storybook
6. Story-Based Puzzle Game that reuses the same story events and prepositions
7. Completion / Rewards

## Digital Storybook narrative

The storybook is the core learning section. It is not a decorative interstitial.

Story events used by every downstream question and puzzle:

- Princess Prepo walks **in** the garden.
- She goes **under** the tree.
- She stands **next to** the castle gate.
- She goes **over** the bridge.
- She finds treasure **in** the treasure room.
- She returns **in** the flower garden.

Every story page must keep the target preposition visually highlighted in the existing Princess & the Prepo pink/gold storybook design system.

## Story comprehension contract

Questions must test recall of the story, not unrelated grammar. Examples:

- Where did Princess Prepo go at the tree? → **under the tree**
- Where did she stand at the castle gate? → **next to the gate**
- How did she cross the bridge? → **over the bridge**
- Where did she find the treasure? → **in the treasure room**

## Puzzle contract

Puzzle Game must reconstruct the same story:

- place/match Princess under the tree;
- match Princess next to the gate;
- complete "Princess Prepo goes over the bridge";
- rebuild a story scene from the same journey.

Do not introduce unrelated vocabulary or locations in the retention section.
